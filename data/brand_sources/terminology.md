## Naming and capitalization examples

- Correct: “Try Cardamom Chai at Nook & Kettle.”
- Incorrect: “Try Cardamom Cloud Chai at Nook and Kettle.”
- Correct: “Visit our Baner café.”
- Incorrect: “Visit our new third outlet in Hinjawadi.” The third location is not approved; see `FACT-02`.
