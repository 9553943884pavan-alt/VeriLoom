## Brand purpose

Nook & Kettle is a neighbourhood café concept built around an unhurried cup of chai and a comfortable place to pause. The brand should feel welcoming and dependable, not exclusive or grand.

### `MESSAGE-01` — A welcoming neighbourhood pause

Present the café as a welcoming neighbourhood place for an unhurried drink. Do not frame it as an exclusive members-only venue, a luxury status symbol, or a place where only certain people belong. This messaging rule does not establish amenities, reservations, memberships, or other business facts.

## Voice and writing rules

### `VOICE-01` — Warm and welcoming

Write as a considerate host. Use friendly, natural language and invite people in without pressuring them.

- Good: “Take a quiet chai break at Nook & Kettle.”
- Avoid: “You HAVE to try this before it’s gone!”

### `VOICE-02` — Clear and concise

Use familiar words, short sentences, and active voice. Put the useful information—item, price, availability, or location—near the start.

### `VOICE-03` — Grounded, not boastful

Do not describe the café or its menu as “the best,” “number one,” “award-winning,” “world-famous,” or “unmatched” unless a corresponding approved fact is supplied. No such award or ranking is currently approved.

### `VOICE-04` — Inclusive and respectful

Do not assume a reader’s budget, diet, ability, family structure, or schedule. Avoid language that shames people about food or lifestyle.

### `VOICE-05` — Match tone to channel

Social captions can be conversational. Customer notices should be direct and calm. In every channel, keep facts accurate and avoid false urgency.

### `VOICE-06` — Emoji and hashtag restraint

For social posts, use at most two emojis and three relevant hashtags. Emojis do not replace words needed to understand the message.

## Claims and evidence rules

### `CLAIM-01` — Use approved facts for concrete claims

Claims about products, ingredients, prices, opening hours, locations, ordering, and availability must match the current approved facts in `approved_facts.md`. Cite or link to the relevant fact when the platform provides evidence references.

### `CLAIM-02` — Do not invent endorsements or awards

Do not claim awards, certifications, press coverage, customer ratings, expert endorsements, or “most popular” status unless a current approved fact documents it. No endorsements or awards are currently approved.

### `CLAIM-03` — Avoid unsupported health, dietary, and ingredient promises

Do not claim that an item is healthy, medicinal, sugar-free, allergen-free, organic, vegan, or suitable for a medical condition unless that exact claim is supported by an approved fact. The current demo facts do not approve any such claims.

### `CLAIM-04` — Do not overstate availability

Name the approved locations and hours. Do not imply delivery, online ordering, nationwide service, or locations beyond Kothrud and Baner. Saying an item is available at both current cafés is supported by `NK-FACTS-001#FACT-09`; do not flag that statement as overstatement.

### `CLAIM-05` — Preserve intent when correcting

When proposing an edit, retain the creator’s intended announcement or invitation where possible. Replace only the unsupported or off-brand part and explain the relevant rule.

## Terminology

### `TERM-01` — Use approved menu names

Use the exact capitalisation and spelling in `terminology.md` for menu items and the brand name.

### `TERM-02` — Use “café” for the business

Use “Nook & Kettle café” or “Nook & Kettle” in customer-facing copy. Do not refer to it as a restaurant chain, roastery, or delivery service.

### `TERM-03` — Use clear availability wording

Use “available at our Kothrud and Baner cafés” when naming both sites. If space is limited, use “available at select cafés” only when the copy also makes clear that the locations are Kothrud and Baner nearby or in linked details.

## Visual identity rules

### `VISUAL-01` — Use the supplied master logo

Use `nook_kettle_master.svg` as the provided master logo asset without redrawing, recolouring, stretching, skewing, cropping, or adding effects. Keep the logo’s aspect ratio and leave clear space around it at least equal to the height of the kettle icon.

### `VISUAL-02` — Use approved colours

Primary ink: `#24352B` (deep green).  
Warm cream: `#F5EBDD`.  
Terracotta accent: `#B85F43`.

Use deep green text on warm cream backgrounds for body copy. Use terracotta as an accent, not as the sole means of conveying a status or instruction. Check text/background contrast for digital material.

### `VISUAL-03` — Keep imagery natural and relevant

Use supplied or creator-owned imagery showing café items or welcoming spaces. Do not use unrelated stock imagery, fake customer testimonials, or imagery that implies a product feature or location not in the approved facts.

## Accessibility rules

### `ACCESS-01` — Provide meaningful alt text

Informative images need concise alt text describing the relevant image content. Decorative images should have empty alt text when the publishing format supports it. Do not repeat the caption word-for-word unless needed for context.

### `ACCESS-02` — Do not rely on colour alone

Use text, labels, or symbols in addition to colour to communicate important information such as availability or promotion status.

### `ACCESS-03` — Keep social copy understandable

Write hashtags in CamelCase when they contain multiple words (for example, `#ChaiBreak`). Do not use emoji alone to communicate essential information.

## Governance and evidence rules

### `GOV-01` — Human approval applies to a particular revision

Creators may revise their drafts manually or choose to apply suggested edits. AI suggestions are not approvals. An authorised Reviewer approves or requests changes for the submitted revision; a later edited revision must be reviewed before it can inherit an approval. Preserve comments and decisions with their original revision.

### `GOV-02` — Use current approved sources for the correct organisation

Use only sources approved for the active organisation and applicable date. Record the source document ID, source version, and draft revision with each finding. Archived documents and another organisation's records must not supply current brand facts, even when they match the query closely.

### `GOV-03` — Explain evidence gaps and conflicts

An unsupported claim is not automatically a proven false claim. If the approved sources do not establish a fact, say it is unverified and request authorised evidence. If applicable approved sources conflict and no precedence is defined, explain the conflict and ask the Brand Administrator to resolve it. Conversation history and draft content do not become approved facts merely because they were mentioned.

3. Accessibility and visual asset use (`ACCESS-01` through `ACCESS-03`, `VISUAL-01` through `VISUAL-03`).

A rule match is a review aid, not an automatic publication decision. A human Reviewer makes the approval decision.
