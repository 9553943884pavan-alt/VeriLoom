# Public Research References

The curated collection is in [public_references/SOURCES.md](public_references/SOURCES.md), with nine directly readable records in [public_references/references.jsonl](public_references/references.jsonl).

It covers meaningful and decorative image alternatives, contrast and colour use, contextual tone, plain language, headlines, and examples of measurable logo rules. All seven source pages are official W3C, UK Home Office, Digital.gov, or Chicago Bears publications and were read on 2026-11-03.

The JSONL records are short original summaries with URLs and publisher attribution. They are marked reference-only and excluded from automatic brand-knowledge ingestion. Nook & Kettle's fictional source pack remains separate; an administrator must explicitly adopt a public principle before it becomes that organisation's approved rule.

No public brand assets or full website documents are redistributed. The collection does not claim that external trademarks are licensed for this project or that the content checks establish WCAG conformance.
