# Short User Survey

## Purpose and status

This is a draft questionnaire for collecting real feedback from potential content creators and reviewers. It contains no responses. Do not report results until people have actually completed it. Participation is optional; do not collect confidential company materials or customer data.

## Intro shown to participants

“We are exploring a tool that could help teams check draft content against their approved brand guidance before publication. This short survey asks about your current workflow and what would be useful. Please do not include confidential information, names of customers, or unpublished campaign details. You may skip any question.”

## Questions

1. **Which role best describes your work?**
   - Content creator
   - Reviewer or editor
   - Manager or team lead
   - Brand or communications administrator
   - Other
   - Prefer not to say

2. **How often do you create or review organisation content?**
   - Daily
   - Several times a week
   - About once a week
   - Less often

3. **Which formats do you work with?** Select all that apply.
   - Social posts
   - Advertisements
   - Web pages
   - Email or newsletters
   - Images or graphics
   - Documents or presentations
   - Other

4. **Where do you find the approved rules, facts, wording, and assets you need?**
   - Open response

5. **How easy is it to find the current approved guidance?**
   - Very easy / Somewhat easy / Neither / Somewhat difficult / Very difficult / Not applicable

6. **Which review problems have you encountered in the last six months?** Select all that apply.
   - Incorrect or outdated facts
   - Unsupported promotional claims
   - Inconsistent tone or terminology
   - Incorrect or outdated visual assets
   - Accessibility concerns
   - Unclear ownership or approval status
   - Repeated review comments or rework
   - Other
   - None of these
   - Prefer not to say

7. **What typically happens when a reviewer requests changes?**
   - Open response

8. **How useful would each capability be?** Rate each: Not useful / Slightly useful / Moderately useful / Very useful / Essential.
   - A finding linked to the exact approved rule or fact
   - An explanation of why the draft was flagged
   - A suggested edit that I can accept, change, or ignore
   - Comments and revision history in one place
   - A reviewer chat for follow-up questions
   - A way to see recurring issues

9. **What information would you need before trusting an AI-generated finding?**
   - Open response

10. **What concerns would you have about using such a tool?**
    - Open response

11. **What is one change that would make content review easier for you?**
    - Open response

12. **May we contact you for a short follow-up?**
    - Yes / No
    - If yes, provide contact details in a separate form or contact channel, not in the anonymised response dataset.

## Response handling

- Store response IDs without names or email addresses.
- Keep optional contact details separately and delete them when follow-up is complete.
- Summarise patterns and include the number of responses; do not generalise a small convenience sample to all organisations.
