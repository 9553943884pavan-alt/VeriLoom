# Consent and Privacy Note for Research

## Participant information

This is a solo hackathon research project exploring how teams create, review, and approve content against brand guidance. Research activities may include short interviews, surveys, or feedback sessions on a prototype.

Participation is voluntary. Participants may skip questions or stop at any time. No payment or benefit is promised unless separately communicated. Do not ask participants to disclose confidential policies, unpublished campaigns, customer data, credentials, or materials they are not authorised to share.

## Data to collect

Collect only what is needed to understand the workflow: role category, anonymised notes, survey answers, and feedback on prototype tasks. Avoid collecting names, email addresses, employer identifiers, or recordings unless genuinely needed and explicitly agreed. Keep contact details separate from research notes.

## Use and retention

Use anonymised summaries in the hackathon plan and product decisions. Do not attribute a quote to an identifiable person without permission. Restrict access to the project team, store notes in a private workspace, and delete raw notes, contact details, or recordings when no longer needed or at the end of the project. Follow any participant organisation requirements and applicable data-protection rules.

## Consent script

“Taking part is your choice. I’m collecting feedback for a hackathon project about brand-content review. Please avoid sharing confidential or personal information. I will anonymise notes and use them to improve the prototype and describe the research at a high level. You can skip questions or stop at any point. Do you agree to participate and for me to take anonymised notes?”

- Participant agreed: yes / no
- Note-taking permission: yes / no
- Recording permission (if applicable): yes / no / not requested
- Date:
- Interviewer:

If the participant declines, do not proceed with research questions or retain identifiable research notes.

## Demo data distinction

All Nook & Kettle company details and the draft examples in this project are fictional, synthetic demonstration material. They are not statements about a real company and are not user research findings. Keep this notice attached when sharing or importing these files.
